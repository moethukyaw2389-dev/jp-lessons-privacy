Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the app "[my testing job]" (the "App"), which is used by its owner only, to publish educational Japanese-language lessons (text, images and short videos) to the owner's own Facebook Page "[Japanese Lessons]".

1. Information we access
The App uses a Facebook Page access token that belongs to the App owner, to publish posts and videos to the owner's own Page. The App does not collect, request or access personal information from visitors, followers or any other Facebook users.

2. Information we collect and store
We do not collect, store, sell, rent or share personal data of any user. The only information stored by the App is the owner's own Page ID and access token, which are kept privately on the owner's own computer.

3. Third-party services
The lesson text is generated with the help of AI services (for example Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude). Only the lesson prompts written by the owner are sent to these services. No personal data of Facebook users is sent to them. Text-to-speech audio for videos may be produced with a third-party text-to-speech service using the lesson text only.

4. Data retention and deletion
Because we do not collect personal data from users, there is no user data to retain. If you believe the App has any data about you, or you want information removed, contact us at the email below and we will delete it.

5. Children
The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

6. Changes
We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page.
